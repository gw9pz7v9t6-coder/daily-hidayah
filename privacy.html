Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

The short version

  • Daily Hidayah works on your iPhone. Your favourites, history and settings stay on your device.
  • No ads. No analytics or tracking tools. No account or sign-up.
  • We never ask for your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • If you buy an optional extra, the purchase is handled by Apple, and our purchase service RevenueCat receives the purchase details needed to unlock it. We never see your card details.

Who we are

Daily Hidayah is made by the Daily Hidayah team, an independent developer based in Melbourne, Australia. Contact: [YOUR EMAIL]

What stays on your device

The app stores the following only on your iPhone (in the app's own storage, shared with its widgets):

  • the ayat you save as favourites;
  • the history of ayat you've been shown;
  • your settings (text size, appearance, widget options, reminder time).

This information isn't sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or a computer, this data may be part of that backup, which Apple's or your computer's terms cover, not ours.

Quran text and context notes

All Quran text is bundled inside the app; nothing is downloaded to show you the daily ayah. The content comes from:

  • English translation: Talal Itani, ClearQuran.com.
  • Arabic text: Tanzil Project (tanzil.net), CC BY 3.0.
  • Context notes (on some verses): short word-for-word excerpts of Al-Mukhtasar fi Tafsir al-Qur'an (English), credited to the Tafsir Center for Quranic Studies and QuranEnc.com.

Reading this content doesn't send any data anywhere. Tapping a credit link opens that website in Safari, where that site's own privacy policy applies.

Daily reminder (notifications)

The reminder is off until you turn it on. If you do, iOS asks for permission, and the app schedules a local notification on your iPhone with that day's ayah. It isn't sent through our servers (we don't have any), and no push-notification token is collected. You can turn it off in the app or in iOS Settings at any time.

Widgets

Widgets read the day's ayah from the app's on-device storage. They make no network requests.

Optional purchases (RevenueCat)

The daily ayah, all widgets, favourites, history and sharing are free. Optional extras are sold as a subscription or a one-time purchase through Apple's App Store.

  • Apple processes the payment. We never receive your name, Apple Account email, or payment card details. Apple's privacy policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • RevenueCat, Inc. is the service we use to check purchases and unlock extras. When the app starts and when you buy or restore a purchase, RevenueCat receives:
    • a random, anonymous app user ID that RevenueCat creates on your device (not linked to your name or email);
    • your purchase history and App Store receipt or transaction information;
    • basic technical details such as device type, operating system, app version, and your locale and currency code;
    • the time you last opened the app.
  • RevenueCat uses this to check that purchases are valid, prevent fraud, restore purchases, and show us anonymous purchase statistics (such as how many people subscribed). We don't use it for advertising and we don't share it with advertisers.
  • We don't set a custom user ID, collect your email, or turn on RevenueCat's advertising or attribution integrations.
  • RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers (IDFA).
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools in the app.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of data.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data.

Information from Apple

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us anonymous, combined statistics (such as installs and crash reports). This is controlled by you in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements and covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app has no accounts, chat or user-generated content, and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete the app to remove all data stored by it on your device.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.
  • For purchase data held by RevenueCat, contact us at [YOUR EMAIL] with the anonymous ID shown in the app (if available) and we'll ask RevenueCat to delete it. You may also have rights under laws such as the Australian Privacy Act, the GDPR, or US state privacy laws (for example California's CCPA); contact us to use them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, mention it in the app's release notes.
