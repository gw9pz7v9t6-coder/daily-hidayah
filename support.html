Support

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Daily Hidayah is a simple iPhone app for a daily ayah and a free full Quran reader. This page answers common questions. If you need more help, email [YOUR EMAIL].

How do I add a widget?

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit (or +) at the top, then Add Widget.
  3. Search for Daily Hidayah, pick a size and tap Add Widget.
  4. For the Lock Screen: touch and hold it, tap Customize, then tap the widget area.

In the app's Settings you can choose Arabic, English, or both for the widget. A favourites-only widget is an optional extra.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Daily Hidayah, go to Settings, and tap Restore purchases. You can also restore from the paywall. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so use the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside the app. On your iPhone, go to Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions, choose Daily Hidayah, and cancel. You can cancel anytime. The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial; you'll get a reminder before you're billed.

What is free, and what is optional?

The daily ayah, the full Quran reader and search, Home and Lock Screen widgets, saving, history, sharing, and browsing ayat by topic are free. There are no ads and no account. Optional extras (sold through the App Store) include extra colour themes, topic filters for the daily ayah and widgets, extra reminder times, extra share-image styles, and a favourites-only widget.
